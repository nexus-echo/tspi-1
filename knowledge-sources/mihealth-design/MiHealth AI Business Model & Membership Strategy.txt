การเขียน
MiHealth AI Business Model & Membership Strategy
Core Philosophy
MiHealth AI and TSPI serve different roles within the ecosystem.
MiHealth AI is not designed to be the primary revenue engine.
Its primary mission is to become the world's largest Personal Health Intelligence Platform by helping individuals collect, organize, and preserve their lifelong health records.
TSPI, on the other hand, serves as the advanced intelligence and intervention layer, providing personalized health analysis, precision health programs, and professional clinical guidance.
Therefore:
MiHealth AI = Platform Layer
TSPI = Intervention & Revenue Layer
Strategic Goal
The most valuable asset of MiHealth AI is not subscription revenue.
The most valuable asset is the long-term relationship with millions of users who trust the platform as the permanent home of their health information.
The objective is to create:
Health Memory
Health Timeline
Health Intelligence
Health Engagement
for every user throughout their lifetime.

TSPI Integration Layer
When the AI identifies health concerns that may require deeper analysis or professional guidance, users may choose to connect with the TSPI ecosystem.
The platform will display:
"If you would like a deeper health assessment or personalized health guidance, you may authorize connection to the TSPI platform and the healthcare professionals of Samutthan Clinic."
This connection is always optional and requires explicit user consent.
Long-Term Vision
MiHealth AI is not simply a medical application.
It is a lifelong personal health intelligence platform.
The mission is to transform fragmented health records into lifelong biological intelligence.
