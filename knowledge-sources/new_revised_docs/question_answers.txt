Q5. What is the standard dose and safety note for each module?

Official Answer

TSPI dosing is determined according to the Network Severity Level rather than disease diagnosis.

The AI should recommend dosage according to the severity classification system.

Final clinical decisions remain under physician supervision.

---

LEVEL 0

Preventive Longevity & Network Optimization

For healthy individuals and longevity programs.

All modules:

2 capsules

twice daily

Administration:

- Morning + Evening

or

Severe biological network dysfunction characterized by high inflammatory activity.

KS Special Protocol

KS follows a separate dosing algorithm.

The dose is determined by bowel function rather than disease severity.

Goal:

Minimum two comfortable bowel movements daily.

Starting dose:

2 capsules before bedtime

or

2 capsules before bedtime
+
2 capsules upon waking

depending on bowel status.

The dose may be increased progressively until bowel targets are achieved.

Safety Rule

TSPI-AI functions as a Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS).

- Biological priorities

Final prescribing authority remains with the supervising physician.
