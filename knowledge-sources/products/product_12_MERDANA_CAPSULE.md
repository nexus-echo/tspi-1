## 🔬 กลไกการออกฤทธิ์ทางชีววิทยา (Mechanistic Activity)
### 1. องค์ประกอบพฤกษเคมี (Botanical Composition)
Beijing grass/Murdannia loriformis (Hassk.) RS Rao & Kammathy., Northern cold rice/Smilax corbularia Kunth., Southern cold rice/Smilax glabra Roxb., Hundred-hole plant/Hydnophytum formicarum Jack., Houttuynia cordata Thunb., Golden wood/Suregada multiflora (A.juss.) Baill., Killer root/Glycosmis pentaphylla (Retz.) DC.

### 2. หน้าที่เชิงกลไกชีวภาพ (Mechanistic Function)
Reduces inflammation of the skin and mucous membranes, supports wound healing and tissue regeneration, modulates immunity in rashes, allergies, and skin sensitivities, reduces the microbial inflammatory burden on the skin and mucous membranes, and is effective in skin-immune-respiratory linkage contexts.

### 3. สารบ่งชี้ชีวภาพหลัก (Key Bioactive Markers LC-MS)
`Murrayafoline A / carbazole alkaloid marker; Astilbin / flavonoid-like marker from Smilax spp.; Quercitrin from Houttuynia cordata; Rutin Glycosmicine / alkaloid marker from Glycosmis pentaphylla.`
