### สรุปฤทธิ์ทางชีวภาพ (Summary of Biological Activity)
Nourishing Blood Tonic is a herbal formula that stimulates bone marrow, increases blood cell production, restores red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets, and protects blood cells from oxidative damage. It is suitable for patients with anemia, chemotherapy patients, those who have lost blood, and elderly individuals with impaired blood cell counts.

*   **ดัชนีการเลือกทำลาย (Selectivity Index):** There is no IC50 data available for this formula.

The formula plays a role in neuroprotective and supportive therapy. 25.
