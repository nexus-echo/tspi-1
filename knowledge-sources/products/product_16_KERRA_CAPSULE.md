### 2. หน้าที่เชิงกลไกชีวภาพ (Mechanistic Function)
Immune balancing + anti-inflammatory + pathogen defense, anti-oxidative and tissue-protective effects. Supportive role in viral/infectious states and chronic inflammatory disorders. Oncological support context through modulation of inflammation, immunity, and tissue resilience, enhancing the microbiome–immune axis and helping to reduce toxic inflammatory load.
