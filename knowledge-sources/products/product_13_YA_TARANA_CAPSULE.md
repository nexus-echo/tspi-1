### 2. หน้าที่เชิงกลไกชีวภาพ (Mechanistic Function)
Reduces muscle and tendon pain and stagnation of energy. Stimulates digestion, absorption, and blood circulation. Reduces inflammatory stagnation in the musculoskeletal system. Helps with bloating and chronic aches. Connects digestive fire with circulation and musculoskeletal repair.
