AM-PUR MIXTURE is a herbal formula for body rejuvenation containing IGF-1 from deer antler velvet. It works to repair cells, stimulate bone marrow, increase muscle mass, and strengthen bones, making it suitable for the elderly, recovering patients, and those seeking energy boosts.

*   **ดัชนีการเลือกทำลาย (Selectivity Index):** There is no specific IC50 value for this formula yet, as it is a tonic formula.
