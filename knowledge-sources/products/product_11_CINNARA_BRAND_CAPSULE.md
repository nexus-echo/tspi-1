## 🔬 กลไกการออกฤทธิ์ทางชีววิทยา (Mechanistic Activity)
### 1. องค์ประกอบพฤกษเคมี (Botanical Composition)
Coriander seeds/Coriandrum sativum L., nutmeg/Myristica fragrances hawkut, mace/Myristica fragrances hawkut, cardamom/Amomum testaceum ridi, cloves/Syzygium aromaticum (L.) Merr. & Lm Perry Exst., Angelica dahurica (Hoffm.) Benth. & Hook. F. Ex Franch. & Sav., Astracticodes lanceosa (Thunb.) DC., Ligusticum sinense oliv., Angelica sinensis (oliv.) Diels., Artemisia palmens wall. Ex DC., and other ingredients.

### 2. หน้าที่เชิงกลไกชีวภาพ (Mechanistic Function)
Regulates gastrointestinal motility and bowel movements, reducing bloating, heartburn, gas, and dyspepsia. Supports digestive secretion and bile flow, helps balance gut-brain-digestive signaling, and reduces inflammation in the upper intestinal mucosa.

### 3. สารบ่งชี้ชีวภาพหลัก (Key Bioactive Markers LC-MS)
`Linalool, Myristicin, Eugenol, Decursin / coumarin-like marker from Angelica dahurica; Borneol / aromatic terpenoid marker from Amomum testaceum.`
