### 2. หน้าที่เชิงกลไกชีวภาพ (Mechanistic Function)
This formula works through several mechanisms simultaneously, as follows: 5.1 Neurotransmitter support: The aromatic herbs and essential oils in the formula tend to help balance serotonergic tone, GABA-related calming pathways, cholinergic balance, and stress-response signaling, thus reducing tension, relaxing the brain, and promoting sleep. 5.2 Neurovascular regulation: The formula helps increase blood flow to the brain, improving oxygen supply to the brain, reducing dizziness, headaches, and vertigo caused by poor circulation, and supporting cognitive clarity. 5.3 Anti-stress/neuroendocrine modulation: It tends to help balance the brain–adrenal–sleep axis, reducing the effects of stress overload on the body. 5.4 Digestive-brain axis: The herbs in the formula also help relieve flatulence and bloating, which reduces the burden on gut-brain stress signaling and indirectly improves sleep. 5.5 Neuroprotection: Experiments on SH-SY5Y neurons showed that the Vatchakron group did not cause cell toxicity at the tested concentrations and tended to increase cell viability compared to the untreated control.

Based on the nature of the formulation and the effects in SH-SY5Y, this module relates to the following pathways: neuroprotective survival signaling, stress-response modulation, oxidative stress buffering, cerebral circulation support, neurotransmitter-balancing pathway, and gut-brain calming pathway. For further research, markers such as BDNF, CREB, GABA-related markers, serotonin-related markers, cortisol-response markers, p-Tau/APP/Rheb should be examined in neurodegeneration models.

In terms of formulations, the dominant compounds should be grouped approximately like this: essential oils/aromatic volatiles, phenylpropanoids, sesquiterpenes, phenolics/coumarins. The key characteristic is not the “cytotoxic fraction,” but rather the neuroactive aromatic fraction + circulatory-support fraction.

### 6. กลไกหลัก 3 เสาหลัก (Main 3 Pillars Mechanism)
The three pillars of H-008 are: Pillar 1: Neuro-regulation, balancing the brain and nervous system; Pillar 2: Sleep restoration, promoting relaxation, easier sleep, and deeper sleep; and Pillar 3: Neurovascular circulation, increasing blood flow to the brain and vital organs.

## 🛡️ ข้อมูลความปลอดภัยและความเป็นพิษ (Safety & Toxicity Profile)
*   **ความเป็นพิษต่อเซลล์ (Cytotoxicity):** From the file Report_KP_CS 30_10_2025.docx, cell viability assays were performed in human SH-SY5Y neurons comparing several formulations: NB1, BI5, Vatcha kron, Bova, Sangwichai, and Psylaza. A key result of Vatcha kron/Vetchakorn was that at tested concentrations of 50, 100, 125, 150, 250, and 500 µg/mL, Vatcha kron tended to increase cell viability by over 100% compared to untreated controls. No significant toxicity was observed in SH-SY5Y cells. Therefore, biologically, it can be concluded that this formulation was not toxic to neurons during the experiment and likely supports cell survival.
*   **ค่าความปลอดภัยสัตว์ทดลอง (LD50 Animal):** The files accessible in this instance did not yet contain a specific LD50 value for H-008. However, given its characteristics as a commonly used oral herbal formulation and its long history of human use, it is considered to have a relatively good therapeutic safety margin. For this field to be accurate to the research literature level, it should be included when specific toxicology reports for the formulation are available.
*   **โปรไฟล์ความปลอดภัย (Safety Profile):** Based on current data, H-008 has a good safety profile as a traditional-use formula neuro-support formula and a non-cytotoxic formula. In particular, the SH-SY5Y data confirms that it did not cause neurotoxicity during the trials.
*   **สถานะงานวิจัย (Research Status):** The research currently supporting H-008 includes: 25.1 Product profile/formula characterization from Vetchakorn product profile and product table; and 25.2 Neuro cell viability assay from the file Report_KP_CS 30_10_2025.docx, which includes Vatcha Kron in the SH-SY5Y experiment. The current status of the evidence is therefore classified as product characterization, mechanistic plausibility, and preliminary cell-based neuro-support evidence.

### สรุปฤทธิ์ทางชีวภาพ (Summary of Biological Activity)
YAVETCHAKORN NO.6 CAPSULE is a module focused on brain and nervous system restoration. It promotes better sleep, reduces stress, improves blood flow to the brain, and supports gut-brain balance. It is safe for nerve cells at the cellular level, as demonstrated by cellular studies. Therefore, this module's focus is on a Brain–Sleep–Stress–Neurovascular Restoration Formula; it's not known for its cytotoxicity against cancer, but it's ideal for those with insomnia, brain fatigue, accumulated stress, brain fog or dizziness due to poor circulation, and those seeking neuro-restorative support.
*   **ดัชนีการเลือกทำลาย (Selectivity Index):** Because there is no IC50 specific to cancer cells, a cancer/normal selectivity index cannot yet be calculated. However, SH-SY5Y data suggest that the formulation is safe for neuronal cells and tends to be more of a supportive than a destructive formula.

### การตีความผลการรักษา (Interpretation)
Although there is no specific IC50 for cancer, from an oncology-support formula perspective, this regimen may be indirectly beneficial in patients with insomnia, stress, nervous fatigue, brain fog, and reduced quality of life. Therefore, it can be positioned as an oncology supportive neuro-sleep formula, not as a primary cytotoxic regimen.
