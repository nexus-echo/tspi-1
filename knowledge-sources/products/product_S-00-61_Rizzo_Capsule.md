*   **การตอบสนองของตัวบ่งชี้ชีวภาพ (Biomarker Response):** TNF-α IL-6 COX-2

Rizzo is a formula for wound healing + immune repair, anti-inflammatory, and anticancer support, suitable for "chronic wounds + immune support + co-occurring cancer".
