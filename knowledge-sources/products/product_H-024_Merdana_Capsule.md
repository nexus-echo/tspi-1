### สรุปฤทธิ์ทางชีวภาพ (Summary of Biological Activity)
Merdana is a herbal formula that helps reduce inflammation, eliminate parasites, reduce infections, treat lymphatic disorders, and stimulate the immune system, increasing NK cells to eliminate abnormal cells.

*   **ดัชนีการเลือกทำลาย (Selectivity Index):** The formula exhibits selective cytotoxicity, showing lower toxicity to normal cells than to cancer cells.
