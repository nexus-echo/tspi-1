## 🔬 กลไกการออกฤทธิ์ทางชีววิทยา (Mechanistic Activity)
### 1. องค์ประกอบพฤกษเคมี (Botanical Composition)
Epimedium grandiflorum, Cervus nippon (deer antler velvet), Panax ginseng (Korean ginseng), Morinda officinalis (Pa Kek Tiang), Cordyceps sinensis (Dong Chong Hae Chao), Atraetylpedes macrocephala (Paeonia lactiflora), Angelica sinensis (Gotu Kola), Astraqalus membranaceus (Huang Qi), Redmania glutiosa (Sek Di), Caesalpinia sappan (Chuang Kiang) and other ingredients.

### 2. หน้าที่เชิงกลไกชีวภาพ (Mechanistic Function)
The formula contains the main herbs Epimedium grandiflorum, Panax ginseng, Angelica sinensis, Astragalus membranaceus, and Caesalpinia sappan. These herbs have the following effects: • Increase cellular energy • Improve blood circulation • Balance hormones.


### 3. สารบ่งชี้ชีวภาพหลัก (Key Bioactive Markers LC-MS)
`Icariin Ginsenosides Ferulic acid Astragaloside Sappan chalcone
`

### 4. เส้นทางโปรตีโอมิกส์ (Proteomics Pathways & Efficacy)
An ancient Chinese herbal formula used to nourish vital energy (Qi & Jing), restore the sex hormone system, enhance sexual performance, and revitalize the body. It is used for those with: • fatigue • decreased sexual performance • low testosterone levels • poor blood circulation. This formula is used in Traditional Chinese Medicine to tonify kidney yang and kidney essence.


### 5. สัดส่วนสารสำคัญสูงสุด (Peak Active Substance Ratios)
None

### 6. กลไกหลัก 3 เสาหลัก (Main 3 Pillars Mechanism)
Epimedium grandiflorum Panax ginseng Angelica sinensis Astragalus membranaceus Caesalpinia sappan
