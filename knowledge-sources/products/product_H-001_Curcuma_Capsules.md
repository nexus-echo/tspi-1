## 🔬 กลไกการออกฤทธิ์ทางชีววิทยา (Mechanistic Activity)
### 1. องค์ประกอบพฤกษเคมี (Botanical Composition)
Turmeric

### 2. หน้าที่เชิงกลไกชีวภาพ (Mechanistic Function)
Turmeric exerts its effects through multiple biological mechanisms, including inhibition of the NF-κB signaling pathway, a key regulator of inflammation in the body; reduction of the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines such as IL-6, TNF-α, and IL-1β; and inhibition of COX-2 and lipoxygenase enzymes involved in gastrointestinal inflammation. Turmeric enhances bile secretion from the liver and gallbladder, resulting in more efficient fat digestion and reducing irritation of the gastrointestinal mucosa. Furthermore, it plays a significant role in balancing the gut microbiome by reducing the growth of pathogens and increasing the diversity of beneficial microorganisms.

## 🩺 ข้อบ่งชี้และการรักษาทางคลินิก (Clinical Indications & Dosage)
*   **โรคและอาการที่ตอบสนองได้ดี (Effective Node Diseases):** Gastric diseases, acid reflux, bloating, stomach ulcers, fatty liver, chronic inflammation in the body.
*   **การตอบสนองของตัวบ่งชี้ชีวภาพ (Biomarker Response):** CRP levels decreased, IL-6 and TNF-α levels decreased, ALT and AST levels improved, and markers of oxidative stress decreased.
*   **ปริมาณและระยะเวลาแนะนำ (Recommended Dose & Duration):** Take 2-4 capsules (500 mg) 3-4 times daily. It is recommended to take them before meals or on an empty stomach, as the herbs are absorbed better when food is absent, leading to higher blood levels of active ingredients and increased efficacy. The herbs in this formula do not irritate the stomach.
*   **ความเสี่ยงการตีกันกับยาแผนปัจจุบัน (Drug-Herb Interaction Risk):** A risk score of 2/10 suggests caution should be exercised in individuals taking anticoagulants or anticoagulant medications.

## 🛡️ ข้อมูลความปลอดภัยและความเป็นพิษ (Safety & Toxicity Profile)
*   **ความเป็นพิษต่อเซลล์ (Cytotoxicity):** Turmeric has low cytotoxicity and has been reported to protect cells from oxidative stress. Some studies have found that curcumin can induce apoptosis in cancer cells without harming normal cells.
*   **ค่าความปลอดภัยสัตว์ทดลอง (LD50 Animal):** The oral LD50 in experimental animals is greater than 5000 mg/kg, classifying it as a highly safe herb.
