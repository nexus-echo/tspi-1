## 🔬 กลไกการออกฤทธิ์ทางชีววิทยา (Mechanistic Activity)
### 1. องค์ประกอบพฤกษเคมี (Botanical Composition)
Northern cold rice/Smilax corbularia Kunth., Three-pronged pruriens/Phlogacanthus sirindhorniae (K.larsen & sslarsen), Blood pruriens/Mackeder & R.Clark., Beijing grass/Parinari anamenis Hance., Black medicine/Aloe vera (L.) Burm. F., Root of the khay tai/Glycosmis pentaphylla (Retz.) DC.

### 2. หน้าที่เชิงกลไกชีวภาพ (Mechanistic Function)
Reduces edema, lymphatic congestion, and inflammatory congestion; supports immune drainage and tissue detoxification; reduces heat toxins and swelling in immune and skin contexts; suitable for chronic inflammation with edema or lymphatic burden; effective in oncology-support, skin-immune, and infection-recovery systems.
