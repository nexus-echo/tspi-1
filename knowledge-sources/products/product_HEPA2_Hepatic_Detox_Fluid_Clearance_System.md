### 2. หน้าที่เชิงกลไกชีวภาพ (Mechanistic Function)
The "Liver Protection – Detoxification – Fluid Clearance" formula works synergistically through detoxification (Phase I–III), autophagy clearance, fluid dynamics regulation, anti-inflammatory and anti-fibrosis effects. It stimulates hepatic detox enzymes (CYP450, GST, UGT), increases bile flow and xenobiotic clearance, promotes autophagy (LC3-II, AMPK), reduces NF-κB/IL-6/TNF-α, and regulates RAAS and fluid balance.
