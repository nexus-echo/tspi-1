### การตีความผลการรักษา (Interpretation)
The formula plays a role in neuroprotective and supportive therapy. 25. Blood Coagulation Modulation: From blood coagulation assay experiments, the clotting time of sample blood (normal blood) was approximately 4.5 minutes, while the BOVA extract was approximately 5.8 minutes. This indicates a slight reduction in platelet aggregation and an increase in microcirculation.
